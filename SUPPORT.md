# Support

## Which channel

**Public issues (this repository)** — bugs and feature requests, from anyone,
licence or not. Public and permanently indexed, so keep keys, credentials,
invoices and customer data out of them.

**Your dashboard** — <https://www.kanunilabs.com/dashboard> — anything tied to
your account: licence keys, seats, allowed domains, invoices, registry
credentials, and support tickets that need to reference them. Tickets opened
here are private and can be attached to a licence, so your whole team sees them.

**Contact form** — <https://www.kanunilabs.com/contact> — pre-sales questions,
or anything that does not fit the two above.

**Security** — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Never a public issue.

## What to expect

| Plan | Channel | Target first response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community (free) | Public issues | Best effort, no commitment |
| Developer | Dashboard tickets | 24 hours, business days |
| Team | Dashboard tickets, priority | 24 hours, business days |
| Enterprise | Dashboard tickets, priority | 24 hours, business days |

Community support is genuinely best-effort: a reproducible bug in a Community
package gets looked at, but there is no queue position to jump. Paid plans get a
tracked ticket with a response target.

## Before opening an issue

1. Search the [existing issues](../../issues?q=is%3Aissue) — including closed ones.
2. Check the [docs](https://www.kanunilabs.com/docs) and the
   [changelog](CHANGELOG.md); the behaviour may have changed in a release.
3. Reproduce on the **latest** version of the package. "It broke after
   upgrading" is useful; "it broke on some old version" usually is not.

## What we cannot do here

- Debug your application. We will happily debug **our component** inside a
  minimal reproduction.
- Discuss anything that requires seeing your licence key or credentials — that
  moves to a dashboard ticket.
- Share source code, internal design documents, or unreleased plans.
